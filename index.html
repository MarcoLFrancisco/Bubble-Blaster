How to play

Move with the Left and Right arrow keys or A and D. Fire with Space. On a touch screen, hold a direction button and tap or hold Fire.

  • Your harpoon travels straight up. Hit large icons to split them; hit the smallest icons to remove them.
  • Avoid bouncing icons. Clear every icon before your three lives or the 90-second timer run out.
  • Use the start/restart button to begin a fresh attempt.
